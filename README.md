# 🍦 ABC Ice Creams Sales Forecasting and Consumer Behaviour Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

This project presents an end-to-end Business Intelligence and Machine Learning solution developed for **ABC Ice Creams**, an India-based ice cream manufacturer and distributor. The objective is to analyse historical sales patterns, understand consumer purchasing behaviour, identify key demand drivers, and forecast future sales to support strategic business decision-making.

Using a realistic synthetic dataset based on Indian weather conditions, seasonal trends, festivals, customer behaviour, and regional characteristics, the project applies data analytics and predictive modelling techniques to generate actionable business insights.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

ABC Ice Creams aims to improve:

* 📈 Sales forecasting accuracy
* 📦 Production planning
* 🏪 Inventory management
* 📣 Marketing effectiveness
* 🌍 Regional expansion strategies
* 🔮 Demand prediction and resource allocation

The company requires a data-driven approach to anticipate future demand and optimize operational decisions.

---

## 🚀 Project Objectives

* Analyse historical ice cream sales patterns.
* Identify factors influencing sales performance.
* Study consumer purchasing behaviour and preferences.
* Perform Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA).
* Develop machine learning models for sales forecasting.
* Predict future demand across products, seasons, and regions.
* Generate strategic business recommendations.

---

## 🗂️ Dataset Description

A synthetic dataset was created to simulate realistic ice cream sales across India.

### 📋 Features Included

| Feature       | Description                                          |
| ------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| Date          | Transaction date                                     |
| Temperature   | Daily average temperature (°C)                       |
| Sales         | Daily sales revenue (₹)                              |
| Day Type      | Weekday / Weekend                                    |
| Season        | Summer / Monsoon / Winter                            |
| Month         | January – December                                   |
| Event         | Festivals, Holidays, School Vacations                |
| Time Slot     | Morning / Afternoon / Evening / Night                |
| Location Type | Urban / Rural                                        |
| Product       | Vanilla, Chocolate, Strawberry, Butterscotch, Coffee |
| Units Sold    | Number of units sold                                 |

**📌 Dataset Size:** 7,305 Records

## 🧠 Behavioural Analytics Findings

### 🌡️ Temperature Influence

* Sales increase significantly as temperature rises.
* Summer months generate the highest demand.

### 🎉 Festival Impact

* Festivals and vacation periods create noticeable sales spikes.
* Event-based marketing opportunities significantly influence revenue.

### 🏙️ Urban vs Rural Consumption

* Urban markets consistently outperform rural markets.
* Higher purchasing power and product accessibility drive demand.

### ⏰ Time-Based Purchasing Patterns

* Evening sales are the highest among all time slots.
* Weekend demand exceeds weekday demand.

### 🍫 Product Preferences

* Chocolate and Butterscotch are the highest-performing flavours.
* Consumer demand is concentrated around classic and premium flavours.

## 🤖 Machine Learning Models

The following models were developed and evaluated:

1. Linear Regression
2. Decision Tree Regressor
3. Random Forest Regressor
4. Gradient Boosting Regressor

### 📏 Evaluation Metrics

* R² Score
* RMSE (Root Mean Squared Error)
* MAE (Mean Absolute Error)
* MAPE (Mean Absolute Percentage Error)

### 🏆 Best Performing Model

**Random Forest Regressor**

The Random Forest model delivered the highest predictive accuracy and effectively captured seasonal, behavioural, and environmental influences on sales.

## 💡 Key Business Insights

* 🌡️ Temperature is the strongest driver of ice cream sales.
* ☀️ Summer contributes the highest annual revenue.
* 🎉 Festivals create significant demand surges.
* 🏙️ Urban markets generate greater sales than rural markets.
* ⏰ Evening is the highest-performing sales period.
* 🍫 Chocolate and Butterscotch are the most profitable flavours.
* 🤖 Random Forest provides the most accurate sales predictions.

## 📌 Business Recommendations

### 📦 Inventory Planning

* Increase inventory levels before summer and festival seasons.

### ⚙️ Production Optimization

* Prioritize production of high-demand flavours such as Chocolate and Butterscotch.

### 📣 Marketing Strategy

* Launch targeted promotional campaigns during Diwali, Holi, and Summer Vacation periods.

### 🌍 Regional Expansion

* Focus expansion efforts on high-performing urban markets.

### 📉 Demand Management

* Utilize predictive forecasting models to reduce stockouts and excess inventory.
